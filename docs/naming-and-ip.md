## Before commercial branding or spending

1. Search exact and confusingly similar/phonetic marks in [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) and relevant national registers, including [IP India](https://ipindia.gov.in/). Coverage is not universal; national/regional searches and professional advice may be needed.
2. Check relevant goods/services classification with qualified guidance, including software/SaaS and research information services as applicable; do not assume a class number guarantees clearance.
3. Verify domain and handle availability through authorized services. No need to purchase a domain for a locally runnable hackathon product.
4. Resolve conflict or obtain qualified advice before investing in commercial branding. A different coined brand remains possible without changing product architecture.
5. User authorizes purchases, filings and agreements. Engineering prepares evidence and changes branding if needed.

WIPO explicitly notes that its database may need to be supplemented by national/regional registers and trademark advice. We therefore cannot responsibly promise “not used anywhere else.”

## Product IP, source material and hackathon publication

Original code/design can be created for this project while respecting third-party licenses. Maintain dependency/license inventory and attribution; do not copy competitor UI/text/code/assets. Competitor workflows are comparison inputs, not templates to duplicate.

Public records and accessible articles are not automatically unrestricted for redistribution. Keep provenance and minimal necessary quotations; respect source access controls, provider terms and deletion requirements. Do not put complete copyrighted article archives, provider response dumps, customer investigations or personal data in the public repository.

The hackathon requires a public repository and AI-tool disclosure. Public code is incompatible with relying on source-code secrecy for that submitted version. Engineering prepares a license recommendation and sanitized repository; the user authorizes release and accepts applicable terms. Do not automatically add an open-source license that grants rights without that decision.

Maintain private credentials and customer data; document third-party usage. No patentability, ownership or freedom-to-operate opinion is claimed. The likely defensibility is execution and verified domain workflow, not exclusive ownership of public facts or generic agent patterns.

## Dependency choice influenced by IP review

The initial PDF-parser proposal was revised: use pypdf (BSD-3-Clause) and pdfplumber (MIT) instead of PyMuPDF, whose official documentation describes AGPL/commercial licensing. This avoids making the default embedded parser depend on that dual-license choice; it does not settle the entire product's licensing. Preserve notices and audit transitive dependencies, containers, Redis/MinIO distributions and hosting terms before release. Actual pinned versions have not been installed or audited.

Primary references: [PyMuPDF licensing](https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/about.html), [pypdf licensing](https://pypdf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/meta/faq.html), [pdfplumber license](https://github.com/jsvine/pdfplumber/blob/stable/LICENSE.txt). [pypdf extraction limitations](https://pypdf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/user/extract-text.html) also require bounded parsing and caution around tables/OCR.
